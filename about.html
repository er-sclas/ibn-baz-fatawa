About this site

This site holds English translations of the fatāwā of Shaykh ʿAbd al-ʿAzīz ibn ʿAbd Allāh ibn Bāz (d. 1420 AH), taken from his official site, binbaz.org.sa. It begins with the names and attributes of Allah, and other chapters are being added. 65 fatwas are translated so far.

How the fatwas are translated

  • Nothing added. No grading of a ḥadīth, no reference, and no honorific appears in the text unless the shaykh wrote it.
  • Nothing removed. Repetitions, reported views, and the closing supplication stay as he gave them.
  • No meaning changed. The attributes of Allah are rendered literally, without taʾwīl: He rose over the Throne, the Hand is the Hand.
  • The translator stays out of the text. No explanation of the translator’s is put into the shaykh’s sentences.

The Arabic

Every fatwa page has an English and an Arabic tab. The Arabic is the shaykh’s text as the page gives it, so a reader can check any sentence. Each page also links to the original on binbaz.org.sa.

Drafts

Every translation is marked Draft until it has been reviewed and approved. This is a working collection, not a published edition. Corrections are welcome.
